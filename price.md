### 2. Yearly Subscription

- **Localization**: English (US)

## Free Tier
- **Price**: Free (No charge)
- **Limits**:
  - Up to 50 contacts
  - 1 pipeline (5 default stages)
  - Up to 20 active deals
  - 3 follow-up reminders per day
  - Basic reports (pipeline total value)
  - 1 user
  - Dark mode

## Pro Tier (Monthly or Yearly)
- **Features**:
  - Unlimited contacts
  - Unlimited deals
  - Unlimited pipelines
  - Unlimited follow-up reminders
  - Up to 5 team members
  - Advanced reports (conversion rates, team performance)
  - Email templates
  - Data export CSV
  - Custom pipeline stages
  - Custom tags and fields
  - Priority support (yearly only)

## Free Trial
- **Duration**: 7 days
- **Type**: Free trial (auto-converts to paid monthly)
