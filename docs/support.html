Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add my first contact?

Tap the Contacts tab at the bottom, then tap the + button in the top right corner. Fill in the contact details and tap Save.

How do I create a deal in the pipeline?

Go to the Pipeline tab and tap the + button. Enter the deal title, value, and select a stage. You can also link it to an existing contact.

How do I move a deal between stages?

Open the deal detail view and tap the branch icon in the top right. Select the new stage from the menu.

How do I set follow-up reminders?

Go to the Today tab and tap + to create a new follow-up. Set the due date, type, and enable the reminder toggle. You'll receive a notification 30 minutes before the due time.

What's included in the free tier?

The free tier includes up to 50 contacts, 10 deals, and 3 follow-up reminders. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited access to all features.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your device, find Pipely CRM, and cancel. You'll retain Pro access until the end of your billing period.

Is my data backed up?

Your data is stored locally on your device. We recommend using the CSV export feature regularly. iCloud sync is available for Pro subscribers.
