Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Pipely CRM is designed with privacy-first principles. We collect minimal data necessary to provide our services:

  • Account Data: Email address and name when you create an account or subscribe.
  • CRM Data: Contacts, deals, activities, and follow-ups you enter. This data is stored locally on your device and optionally synced via your private iCloud account.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics about app usage to improve our product. No personal CRM data is included in analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Pipely CRM service
  • To process subscription payments via the App Store
  • To send follow-up reminders and notifications you've requested
  • To respond to support requests
  • To improve app performance and user experience

3. Data Storage & Security

Your CRM data is stored locally on your iOS device using Apple's SwiftData framework with encryption. If iCloud sync is enabled, data is transmitted and stored using Apple's CloudKit infrastructure with end-to-end encryption. We do not have access to your CRM data on our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: For subscription management and payment processing. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple CloudKit: For optional data synchronization. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual contacts, deals, or activities within the app
  • Uninstalling the app, which removes all local data
  • Contacting us at support@zzoutuo.com to request complete data deletion

6. Children's Privacy

Pipely CRM is a business tool intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data
  • Export your data (available via CSV export in the app)
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email.
