# App Store Metadata

## Promotional Text
Manage your sales pipeline visually. Track contacts, deals, and follow-ups in one simple CRM built for small teams.

## Description
Pipely CRM is the visual sales pipeline manager built for small businesses, freelancers, and sales teams who want to close more deals without the complexity of enterprise CRM software.

See your entire sales pipeline at a glance with our intuitive Kanban-style board. Move deals through stages from Lead to Won with a single tap. Never miss a follow-up again with smart reminders that keep you on track.

KEY FEATURES

Visual Pipeline Board
- Kanban-style view of all your deals across every stage
- Drag deals between stages to update progress instantly
- See total deal value per stage at a glance
- Color-coded stages for quick visual scanning

Contact Management
- Organize all your contacts with company, job title, and tags
- Link contacts directly to deals for full context
- One-tap calling and email from any contact
- Smart search across names, companies, and emails

Smart Follow-Up Reminders
- Schedule follow-ups by call, email, or meeting
- Get notified 30 minutes before each follow-up is due
- See overdue, today, and upcoming follow-ups at a glance
- Mark follow-ups complete with one tap

Deal Tracking
- Track deal value, expected close date, and probability
- Log activities like calls, emails, meetings, and notes
- Quick actions to call or email contacts from deal view
- Full activity history for every deal

Today View
- See all overdue and upcoming follow-ups in one place
- Prioritize your day with smart sorting
- Complete follow-ups directly from the list

Pro Subscription
Upgrade to Pipely Pro for unlimited access:
- Unlimited contacts and deals
- Unlimited follow-up reminders
- Team collaboration for up to 5 members
- CSV data export
- Advanced reports and analytics

Pipely CRM is designed for simplicity. No complicated setup, no steep learning curve. Just open the app and start managing your pipeline. Your data stays on your device with optional iCloud sync, so you always have access even offline.

Whether you are a solo entrepreneur tracking a handful of deals or a small team coordinating sales efforts, Pipely CRM gives you the tools to stay organized and close more deals.
